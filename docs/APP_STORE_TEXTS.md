## Description (max 4000 characters)

```
Water Tracker makes staying hydrated simple, beautiful, and rewarding. Whether you want to build a healthy drinking habit or track your daily water intake with precision, this app gives you everything you need in a clean, intuitive interface.

BEAUTIFUL DASHBOARD
A large circular progress ring shows your daily hydration at a glance. Watch it fill up as you drink throughout the day, turning green with a checkmark when you hit your goal. A day progress bar tracks your active hours so you can pace your intake.

FLEXIBLE WATER INPUT
Add water your way. Use customizable quick action buttons for your favorite amounts — glass, bottle, can, or create your own. Need precision? Open the modal picker and dial in the exact amount. You can also remove entries if you log something by mistake.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
Keep your hydration front and center with native widgets for iOS and Android. See your progress, current streak, and daily goal without opening the app. The medium widget even lets you add water with a single tap, right from your home screen.

11 ACHIEVEMENTS TO UNLOCK
Stay motivated with a built-in achievement system. Earn badges for your first glass, maintaining streaks of 3, 7, 14, and 30 days, hitting weekly and monthly goals, and reaching cumulative milestones of 10, 50, and 100 liters. In-app notifications celebrate every unlock.

DETAILED STATISTICS & TRENDS
Track your progress over time with weekly, monthly, and all-time views. See your average intake, total consumption, goal achievement rate, and current streak. Trend analysis compares your current period to the previous one, so you always know if you're improving.

SMART REMINDERS
Set reminders at 1, 2, or 3-hour intervals during your active hours. Control how many notifications you receive before your next drink. Reminders automatically reschedule after you log water, so they never feel annoying.

FULL DATA CONTROL
Your data is yours. Automatic daily backups keep your history safe. Export everything as JSON for a complete backup, or as CSV for spreadsheet analysis. Import data anytime to restore or migrate between devices.

COMPLETELY CUSTOMIZABLE
Set your daily water goal, glass capacity, and active hours. Add up to 10 custom quick action buttons with your own labels and amounts. Choose your language, date format, and toggle haptic feedback. Make the app truly yours.

PRIVACY FIRST
All data stays on your device. No account required. No data sent to servers. Just you and your water.

Built with love for people who want to drink more water.
```

# Social Challenges with Friends

The app has no social features: no friend challenges, leaderboards or invites.

## Why this is out of scope

Challenges need user accounts, a shared backend and real-time data between users, all of which are out of scope (see `cloud-sync-and-accounts.md`). Motivation in this app comes from personal Goals, Streaks and Achievements rather than competition.
